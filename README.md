# Indo-German Trade & Logistics Analytics Dashboard 📊

## Overview
An end-to-end Power BI business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor and analyze bilateral trade corridors, logistics performance, financial settlements, and anomaly tracking between India and Germany.

## Key Features & Visuals
* **Executive KPI Strip:** Real-time summary cards tracking transaction counts, average settlement latencies, anomaly scores, and total trade values.
* **Geospatial & Temporal Trends:** Interactive map visuals tracking shipping corridors alongside monthly trend and area charts.
* **Granular Breakdowns:** Cross-tabulated data matrices, payment method analyses (SWIFT, Crypto, Wire Transfer), commodity distributions, and country-wise percentage share donut charts.

## Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop, Power Query
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema, Relationships, Calculated Columns
* **Data Analysis Expressions (DAX):** Measures for dynamic KPIs, aggregations, and filtering.
